# Product

FiberCairn: Understand recurring network service problems.

AI service reliability software that analyzes authorized network outage records, customer complaints and maintenance reports to identify recurring service problems.

- Founder and CEO: Ifeanyi Nwachukwu
- Co-founder and CTO: Ibrahim Adesina
- Industry: Telecommunications, Enterprise Software, Machine Learning
- Customers: Internet service providers, telecommunications operators and managed network providers
- Core features: Service incident classification, outage trend analysis, maintenance history correlation, service restoration reporting, reliability dashboards
- MVP: Incident record imports and reliability reports
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, SageMaker AI, CloudWatch, S3, Lambda
- Business model: Subscriptions based on managed locations
- Main action: Request a pilot
