How a FiberCairn pilot runs

  1. Import incident records

    You send twelve months of outage tickets, complaints and work orders. Customer names are removed before storage.

  2. Review findings with your engineers

    Findings arrive each week. Your engineers confirm or dismiss each one, and we tune the classes.

  3. Read the reliability report

    A monthly report per region: repeat outages, restoration times and the fixes your team chose.

What network operations teams ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

What data do we need?

Outage tickets, complaint records and maintenance reports you are authorised to share, as exports, CSV or PDF.

Does it touch our network?

No. FiberCairn reads exported records only and has no access to network equipment.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by the number of managed locations. We quote after the pilot.

Who reviews the findings?

Your engineers. Every finding is confirmed or dismissed by a person.

Where is our data kept?

In an AWS account of its own, in the region you choose, encrypted at rest and in transit.
